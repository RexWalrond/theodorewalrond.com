The Teton Crest Trail, With a Loop Over Paintbrush Divide

By Theodore Walrond

The Teton Crest Trail runs along the west side of Wyoming's Teton Range, and in August 2022 I hiked an extended version of it: about 50 miles and 9,000 feet of gain, closing the loop over Paintbrush Divide instead of walking out the standard finish.

The route

The highlights come one after another: the long, exposed traverse of Death Canyon Shelf, the open meadows of Alaska Basin, and finally Paintbrush Divide, the high point at about 10,720 feet. The Grand, Middle and South Teton stay in view for most of the trip, which is the whole argument for doing this route.

Conditions

  • Exposure. Death Canyon Shelf is a high bench with big drops, beautiful and best done in good weather.
  • Snow on the divide. Even in August, snow can linger near Paintbrush Divide, so it pays to check conditions before committing.
  • Bear canister required. Grand Teton National Park requires approved food storage in the backcountry.

Of all the trips on my list, this is the one I'd recommend for the views per mile. Notes on the others, from the Four Pass Loop to Glacier and the High Sierra, are in my field notes.
